## Foundation grammar tree

The new course has twelve original lessons and 36 quiz questions plus 25 practice solutions. Each lesson links the relevant Whitney chapters directly. The original 1889 work is marked public domain on Wikisource; modern site content and interface terms are distinct. No contemporary course text, audio, or images were copied.

- Cases: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Sanskrit_Grammar_(Whitney)/Chapter_IV
- Nouns: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Sanskrit_Grammar_(Whitney)/Chapter_V
- Pronouns: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Sanskrit_Grammar_(Whitney)/Chapter_VII

The course uses modern IAST rather than copying Whitney's historical transcription conventions. Accent marks are not taught here. Vowel length and retroflex/aspirated distinctions are retained. Explanations explicitly distinguish seven numbered cases plus the vocative; the phrase “eight cases” is used as introductory shorthand. The noun lessons cover singular case forms and nominative/accusative number contrasts; they do not claim to teach every declension.

Examples preserve word boundaries and sometimes pause forms. Full external sandhi, other tenses, participles, and compounds are beyond the chosen foundation scope. Sanskrit and matching IAST appear with meanings; deliberately incorrect quiz distractors and correction exercises are identified by context and explained in the answers. Ten minutes is a suggested study-and-practice time, not a reading-speed estimate.

Lesson text lives in `scripts/course_content.py`; rendering in `scripts/course_pages.py`; book metadata in `data/learning.json`. Source pages and rights statements were checked on 25 September 2026.
